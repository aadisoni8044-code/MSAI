WORLD & LORE

About Shadow Realm

When the Void Alignment shattered the elemental balance, dark necrotic energy spilled across 10 mythical realms. You play as the last Shadow Hunter, equipped with ancient elemental magic and high-tech weaponry.

Combine high-mobility platforming, wall jumps, and dash dodges with tactical tower defense mechanics to cleanse the corrupted lands and hold the line against infinite zombie waves.

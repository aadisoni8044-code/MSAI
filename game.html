ABOUT THE GAME

Set in an ancient realm fractured by dark elemental corruption, Shadow Forest: Zombie Outbreak immerses players into high-octane combat, platforming puzzles, and brutal survival wave combat.

As an elite shadow guardian, you must harness 10 elemental magical attributes (Forest, Fire, Water, Ice, Desert, Thunder, Poison, Sky, Shadow, and Crystal) to defeat demonic titans and withstand the relentless undead outbreak.

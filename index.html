HOW TO PLAY

Desktop Controls

  • A / ◄ : Move Left
  • D / ► : Move Right
  • W / ▲ / Space : Jump / Double Jump
  • J / X : Sword Attack
  • ESC / P : Pause Game

Mobile Touch Controls

  • On-Screen D-Pad: Tap Left/Right arrows to walk
  • ▲ Button: Jump (Tap again in mid-air to Double Jump)
  • ⚔️ Button: Melee Sword Strike
  • ⏸️ Button: Pause Menu

Adventure Tips

✨ Collect glowing Ancient Runes to increase your score.

🛡️ Pass glowing Stone Checkpoints to save your progress in the level.

👾 Watch out for forest enemies! Strike them with your sword or jump over them.

👑 Defeat the Ancient Forest Guardian at the end of the grove to claim victory!

ABOUT THE GAME

"Whispers of the Ancient Grove"

Embark on an enchanting journey through a dark, mysterious forest filled with giant ancient trees, hanging vines, atmospheric mist, and ancient magical runes.

Built with modern Canvas API, Web Audio synthesizer, and responsive layout for desktop and mobile play.

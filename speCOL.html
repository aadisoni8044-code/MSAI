How to Play

Parachute onto the island, scavenge for weapons, armor, and healing kits, and eliminate all enemies to be the last survivor standing!

  • Movement: WASD or Arrow Keys (Shift to Sprint)
  • Aim & Fire: Mouse pointer to aim, Left Click to shoot
  • Switch Weapons: Keys 1, 2, 3 or Mouse Scroll
  • Interact / Pickup: Key E or F
  • Reload: Key R
  • Safe Zone: Stay inside the blue circle! The zone shrinks over time. Outside players take constant damage.
